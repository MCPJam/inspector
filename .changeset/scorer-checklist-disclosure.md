The scorer table on suite settings and a case's checks page reads as a checklist with progressive disclosure. An off row says only its name. Once on, its Gate / Warn / Report control sits on the row, a rule that carries a number shows it under its name, and the name grows a chevron when there is an editor behind it (the rule's own fields, or the judge's threshold); a rule that is nothing but its kind opens nothing. The stage strip, the "Evaluators by stage" heading and hint, and the per-stage questions are suite-settings furniture and no longer render on a case page. Runner-measured stages are named as checks ("Successful connection", "Tools listed by the server"). Where a role cannot be changed — an inherited rule on a case page, or a backend without check policy — the chip's tooltip says why.
