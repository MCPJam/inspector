The evaluator list on suite settings and a case's assertions page reads as a checklist with progressive disclosure. An off row says only its name. Once on, its Required / Advisory control sits on the row, an assertion that carries a number shows it under its name, and the name grows a chevron when there is an editor behind it (the assertion's own fields, or the judge's threshold); an assertion that is nothing but its kind opens nothing. The stage strip, the "Assertions" heading and hint, and the per-stage questions are suite-settings furniture and no longer render on a case page. Runner-measured stages are named as what they measure ("Successful connection", "Tools listed by the server"). Where a role cannot be changed — an inherited assertion on a case page, or a backend without assertion policy — the chip's tooltip says why.
