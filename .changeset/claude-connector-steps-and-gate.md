Every step of Claude's connector form is graded from a submission's answers. Five new checks: `claude.submission.use-cases` (one to three use cases, each with its example prompt, and the prerequisites), `claude.submission.company` (company name, an https website, contact name and email), `claude.submission.authentication` (the requirement and the OAuth client are answered), `claude.submission.data-handling` (API ownership, personal health data and sponsored content are answered and confirmed rather than left at the portal's default) and `claude.submission.test-instructions` (test setup instructions when users sign in, from Claude's review criteria). `claude.submission.attestations` also asks for the seven acknowledgments to be confirmed and lists the ones not ticked, and `claude.submission.declared-auth-mode` holds the Authentication answers against the server's sign-in when the run saw it. A problem the portal refuses is reported in its words; a rule that is MCPJam's own says so. Every Claude check has exactly one placement on the connector flow, naming the answers it reads and what it needs, and a choice field whose options were never captured (Categories) carries `freeEntry: true`.

`directoryVerdict(findings, { checkGate, path })` decides a run under a submission's gate. By default every violated `required` or `runtime-blocker` check fails the run; `warnOnly` takes a check out and `failOn` adds any check. A gated check with no current verdict (not evaluated, blocked, stale or never reported) makes the run `incomplete`. Checks the path cannot evaluate (the intrusive probes on the hosted path, and the RFC 8707 resource check without a recorded OAuth sign-in) are listed in `notCheckedOnPath` with the reason and stay out of the default gate. It is exported from `@mcpjam/sdk` and `@mcpjam/sdk/browser`. The `status` that `gradeClaudeReadiness` reports is unchanged.

Claude citations point at the current pages under `https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/`; the directory overview and the verification page have their own URLs. Pages read on 2026-11-13 carry that date as their `snapshotDate` and `revision`, and `CLAUDE_POLICY_SNAPSHOT_DATE` is 2026-11-13; `isPolicyCorpusVerified()` counts only the sync script's hashes. OpenAI's guidelines citation moves from the `app-guidelines` page to `plugin-guidelines`.
