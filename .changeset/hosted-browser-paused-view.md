A cloud browser that paused because nobody used it now shows "Browser paused" over its last picture, in the browser view and in "Full desktop (the old viewer)" alike. It wakes only when you click it, press a key in it, or choose Resume; watching it or switching back to its tab does not. When the agent's next browser command wakes it, the view comes back by itself within about ten seconds.
