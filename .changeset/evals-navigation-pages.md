Behind the `evaluate-nav-enabled` flag, every layer of an eval gets its own page and address: Suites and Runs tabs on Evaluate, a read-only Suite page, Suite settings with Settings, Test cases and Default evaluators tabs, and Suite run, Test run and Iteration pages with breadcrumbs and Back. Test cases open in a step editor with an execution preview, can be copied to another suite, and a first-visit tutorial explains the four layers. Old Evaluate addresses redirect to their new pages when the flag is on.
