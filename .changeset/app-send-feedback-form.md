Send feedback from the app. Settings → Support has a **Send feedback** form for telling the MCPJam team about a bug, a missing capability, or something confusing; it says under the form that the text goes to the MCPJam team and is kept for 180 days. Signed-out visitors are asked to sign in instead. On the hosted app, an MCPJam error's technical details now include a **Report this** link that opens the form with the failing request's id and error code filled in. One report per opening of the form: a double-click or a retry never files it twice.
