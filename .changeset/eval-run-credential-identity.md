Eval runs that record their accounts now show each account's credentials: who uploaded or signed in its token and when, when it expires, and whose it is, from the server's profile tool or else from the token's own claims. Connecting a server with a shared or uploaded account captures its identity from the profile tool, for the runs that follow. Shown where eval account selection is turned on for your account.
