Say why a User Testing insights view is empty, and let a reader analyze a session now. The Session flow and Findings tab used to read "This can take a few minutes" whatever the reason, and draw a flow of placeholder bars before any theme existed. They now name the reason from the analysis summary: no sessions yet, waiting for a session to go quiet (with the time analysis starts), analyzing N sessions, the daily limit (with when it resumes), a guest study, a failure, outcomes still to come (with when they fill in), or themes still being grouped. Where waiting, pending outcomes, a guest study or a retryable failure is the reason, members get an **Analyze now** button that treats the study's sessions as finished. A session's detail view has the same button while that session is waiting or its outcome is still pending. Early themes, proposed from fewer than eight sessions, are labelled as such under the Session flow title. Needs the matching backend release; older backends keep the previous copy.
