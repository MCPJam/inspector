New platform operation `draft_climb_change` and its `draftClimbChange` client method: a model's rewrite of one tool's description (and, on request, its title) from the failed steering cases of a climb, returned as a draft and as the merge patch `update_mock_server` takes. It saves nothing and starts no run. `mcpjam cloud climbs draft` is the CLI command.
