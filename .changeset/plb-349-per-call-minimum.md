On the free daily plan, MCPJam models are locked as soon as the credits left can't cover one model call, not only at 0. The tooltip says "Not enough MCPJam credits · a model call needs at least 25", and Run stays disabled for a suite whose models are all MCPJam-hosted. A saved own-key model stays usable, and the lock lifts at the daily reset without a reload.
