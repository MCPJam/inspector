The OAuth Debugger's Guide view and the XAA Debugger's Flow view can copy a range of steps in one go. Click "Select step" next to "Copy all", click a step to select it, shift-click another to select every step in between, then click "Copy N steps". The steps are copied in flow order, with the same redaction as the existing copy buttons.
