The Readiness page shows a submission's test plan on Claude's Test & launch step, marked as MCPJam's. It names the saved revision, who saved it and when, and the issues that keep it from running, each on the tool or case it names. Without a saved plan it says that runs call none of the server's tools, and offers to draft one with every tool, destructive calls last. A person can edit each case's arguments and expected refusal, exclude a tool with a reason, and change the budget, then save a new revision; a save refused because another revision was saved offers to load that revision.
