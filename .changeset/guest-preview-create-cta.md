Signed-out visitors on the Swarms and User Testing previews now see one button, "Create new swarm" or "Create new study", instead of separate "Create account" and "Sign in" buttons. It opens a dialog that says what a first swarm or study shows you, with "Create free account" and "Sign in". After signing up or in from the tab or its create page, the visitor lands on the create page, in the same project. From a shared swarm or study link, they return to that link.
