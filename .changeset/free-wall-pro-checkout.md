An owner of a Free organization who runs out of MCPJam credits can now buy Pro from the credit wall. The wall shows Pro's monthly and annual prices, and "Upgrade to Pro" opens Stripe Checkout with the selected interval. "Use my own API key" and "Compare plans" become links. If checkout can't open, the wall says so and stays up. Members and admins still see "Ask an owner", and the swarm wall is unchanged.
