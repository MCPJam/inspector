Adds `mcpjam cloud mocks`: `create`, `update`, `get` (bounded pages: a summary, a list of names with `--family` and `--search`, one entry with `--name`, one field with `--path`, long strings in chunks with `--cursor`), `patch` (one path against `--expected-version`), `refresh` (previews the real server's changes, `--apply` saves them) and `policy get` and `policy set`.
