New Swarm sizes a swarm to what the organization's credits and the user's free swarm conversations can pay for. When they cover less than the default swarm, Generate writes fewer goals (up to 3 personas), and a notice names the balance and the most conversations that fit. At 0, generation does not start and the notice offers more credits. On Confirm, the total reads "N of max conversations", each add control stops before it would pass the max, and Continue is blocked above it. Without a complete estimate there is no cap, as before.
