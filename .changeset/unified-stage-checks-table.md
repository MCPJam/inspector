Suite settings and a case's checks page now render one scorer table. The standalone "Checks by stage" checkbox list is gone; its On/off control is the first column of the numbered 01–06 table, which lists every standard check a stage could run as an off row with its preset criterion and role. On the suite page a box switches a rule in or out of the suite's list. On a case page the suite's rules are listed as inherited — read-only, switchable off by standard-check family — above the case's own, which edit in place; the judge row is the case's skip flag.
