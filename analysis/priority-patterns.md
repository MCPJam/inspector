## Three tractable fixes and bounded improvement claims

1. **Command-specific usage remedy.** Adds the relevant `mcpjam … --help` invocation without echoing arguments/secrets, preserving exit semantics. Patched `healthy__FC2asax` used the emitted `cloud eval status --help`, corrected `--run-id` to `--run`, and retrieved the passed report ([3252021f9e8040aab3b3830609deb1a9](../patched-priority-v13--healthy__FC2asax/commands/0008-3252021f9e8040aab3b3830609deb1a9/event.json) → [9b13d768595441c4bed4bda215c1f6eb](../patched-priority-v13--healthy__FC2asax/commands/0016-9b13d768595441c4bed4bda215c1f6eb/event.json)). Two patched trials encountered the remedy; one visibly completed that exact correction, one used other account context without correcting `cloud organizations`. Baseline already sometimes recovered independently; do not claim a causal success lift.
2. **Pre-run Eval credit help.** Documents hosted product credits versus agent-provider costs, balance location, unavailable guaranteed CLI estimate, and iterations versus model steps. Exposure was only 1/8 patched trials that fetched CLI (`healthy__Am8udgv`, events [07ce925c2756470aa05a2bd0d9522f35](../patched-priority-v13--healthy__Am8udgv/commands/0003-07ce925c2756470aa05a2bd0d9522f35/event.json), [ef5ae689fd31458aa10f055100872f13](../patched-priority-v13--healthy__Am8udgv/commands/0062-ef5ae689fd31458aa10f055100872f13/event.json), [8b0fd458ffd4467b9cf84c69c6b732e3](../patched-priority-v13--healthy__Am8udgv/commands/0042-8b0fd458ffd4467b9cf84c69c6b732e3/event.json)). No D credit trial displayed this help. The wording/regression test is verified; behavioral improvement in pre-run cost understanding is not demonstrated.
3. **Role/plan-aware fee guidance.** Preserves wire code/details, names Organizations > Billing, conditions owner/member/plan actions, avoids claiming balance or price, and asks to retry after resolution. Both actually exposed patched credit trials reflected these concepts and resumed the same work. Baseline exposed trial reports missing exact page. This is the strongest observed copy improvement; sample size is two patched versus one baseline.

All three fixes have focused deterministic regression checks and captured before/after CLI outputs. They do not add a billing API, alter charging, retry automatically, or establish real plan-role coverage.
