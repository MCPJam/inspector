# Feature Spotlight

A one-time card pinned to the control it introduces.

**Use it** to point someone at a new feature they can try right now, from where
they are, with one action that starts it.

**Don't use it** for errors or warnings (use an alert or ErrorCard), for help on
hover (HoverCard), or for anything shown more than once.

Open it after the page settles and treat any close as final. It doesn't move
focus, and Escape or an outside click closes it. The product decides when it
opens; the component only draws it.
