# App banner

A full-width strip at the top of the app about the account or the session, not
the page in front of you.

**Use it** for a state that follows the reader everywhere until it's fixed: a
failed payment, an expired session, a refused guest session.

**Don't use it** for a problem on one page (use an alert or ErrorCard), for
feedback on an action (a toast), or to announce a feature (Spotlight).

The message is left aligned and the actions sit at the right edge. Write the
problem first and the fix second, and bold only the problem.

<Canvas of={Stories.Prominent} />

## Emphasis

**Prominent** asks the reader to fix something. It has a warning rail, an alert
icon and the fix as a button. Don't make it dismissible while the problem
blocks work.

**Quiet** tells someone who can't fix it. It has no fill and no action, and it
is dismissible for the session. A failed payment shows prominent to billing
managers and quiet to members.

**Neutral tone** is for a session problem that isn't a warning about the

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**

- Keep it in page flow above the header, unless it must cover a screen that
can't render (session recovery banners are fixed for that reason).
- Clear it from live state, so it goes away when the problem does.

**Don't**

- Center the text or put the action in the middle of the strip.
- Stack two banners. Show the one the reader can act on.
- Use a literal amber or red. Use the `warning` tone.
