## Billing

### Payment history

The **Payment history** card on the org billing page lists your recent credit top-ups. Each row shows:

| Column | Details |
| - | - |
| Date | When the top-up was processed |
| Amount | Amount charged in USD |
| Status | `Succeeded`, `Pending`, or `Failed` |
| Receipt | Link to the Stripe-hosted receipt (opens in a new tab) |

If a receipt isn't available yet the cell shows **Processing** (for pending charges) or **Not available** (for succeeded charges without a receipt link).

To add credits, click **Top up** — either from the empty state when you have no history, or from the credit balance card above.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Free plan organizations cannot purchase credits directly.** If your organization is on the Free plan, the Top up dialog shows a message that only an organization owner can upgrade the plan, with a **Compare plans** button. Clicking it closes the dialog and opens your organization's Plans settings so you can upgrade.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Desktop app:** When you confirm a top-up in the desktop app, checkout opens in your default system browser and the dialog closes automatically. Your credit balance updates in the app once the purchase completes — no redirect back into the app is required.

### Spend budget

An owner or admin can cap what the whole organization spends on MCPJam-billed
work — chat, evals, judges and computer time — for each billing period, from
**Organization → Budget**.

* Set the cap in dollars, and choose the percentages to be warned at. The
  default warns at 80%; reaching the cap always notifies, whether or not you
  list it.
* Warnings go to the organization's owners and admins in the notifications
  bell, and to the owner by email.
* **When the budget is reached**, new chat turns, eval runs and computer starts
  are refused for the whole organization. The refusal says the budget is
  reached and that an owner or admin can raise it — buying credits does not
  clear it. Work already in flight finishes, and running computers keep going
  until they hibernate.
* A scheduled eval that hits the budget pauses its schedule rather than
  retrying into the same cap every window; it resumes once you raise the cap or
  the period rolls over.
* Runs that use **your own API keys** are never counted against the budget and
  never blocked by it. Neither is the free daily allowance, which is not
  credits.
* The window is your organization's own billing period: the subscription cycle
  for team and enterprise plans, and the calendar month otherwise. Raising or
  lowering the cap mid-period does not reset what has already been spent.

Any member can see the budget and how much of it is left; only owners and
admins can change it. Every change is recorded in the organization's audit log
with who made it and what it was before.

The same budget is readable and writable from the API at
`/v1/organizations/{organizationId}/spend-budget`.

### Managing billing details
### Out of MCPJam credits

`provider/mcpjam_limit_daily` · `provider/mcpjam_limit_monthly` · `provider/mcpjam_limit` · `provider/mcpjam_limit_insufficient`

MCPJam refused the call before it reached a provider. For the daily, monthly,
and general codes, the account's own allowance is spent.
`provider/mcpjam_limit_insufficient` means the balance is not empty but is below
what the request could cost. Which allowance depends on the plan: free accounts
share a daily bucket per organization, paid plans spend a monthly allowance that
renews with the billing period.

`provider/mcpjam_limit_insufficient` means the balance is not empty but is below
what the request could cost. MCPJam reserves a request's worst-case cost before
it runs, so a cheaper model or a shorter conversation can fit where this one did
not.

**Next steps**

* **Free plan (owner)** — The limit dialog shows **Compare plans** to upgrade, and **Learn more about BYOK** to understand how provider keys and MCPJam credits work separately.
* **Free plan (admin or member)** — The dialog shows a **Request upgrade** link to email an organization owner.
* **Paid plan (owner or admin)** — The dialog shows **Buy credits** to add shared credits to your organization.
* **Paid plan (member)** — The dialog shows a **Request credits** link to email an organization owner.
* Add your own API key under **Settings → LLM Providers** for chat (BYOK does not cover Swarm, Evals, or other MCPJam-billed features).
* If your organization uses its own keys for all AI features, model tokens are billed by its providers instead; see [Organization AI key policy](#organization-ai-key-policy).

**Swarm and Eval runs**

When credits run out during a Swarm or Eval run, blocked work stops and you receive one credit-exhaustion prompt per run, or one per swarm when a swarm launches several runs at once. Completed results are preserved. Buying credits does not automatically restart the run — start a new run once credits are available.

> [!WARNING]
>
> Bring-your-own-key covers chat. Swarm persona generation and persona-driver
> turns are billed to MCPJam and have no personal BYOK path, so adding a key
> does not lift this limit for a swarm. An organization that uses its own keys
> for all AI features runs those turns on its default AI model instead, and its
> providers bill their tokens; see
> [Organization AI key policy](#organization-ai-key-policy).

### Credits temporarily held

A swarm session can stop with **Credits temporarily held**. The balance is not
empty: other requests from your organization were in flight and held the
remaining credits until they finish. No credit prompt opens for it and nothing
needs buying.

**Next steps**

* Run the session again once your other sessions have finished.
* Start fewer sessions at once if it keeps happening.
