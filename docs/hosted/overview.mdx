## Quick start

1. Go to [app.mcpjam.com](https://app.mcpjam.com).
2. Paste your MCP server's HTTPS URL and click **Connect**.
3. Start inspecting tools, resources and prompts.

## Why use the hosted app?

- **No installation**: Open a browser and start testing.
- **Always up to date**: You're always on the latest version without running `npx @mcpjam/inspector@latest`.
- **Shareable**: Share server URLs with your team. Everyone can inspect the same server without local setup.

## Share your MCP server

Send a link to your MCP server or app the same way you'd share a Google Doc. Anyone with an MCPJam account who opens it can start chatting with the server in their browser.

## Usage limits

Chat and the other AI features in the hosted app use daily credits.

- **Guests** get a free daily allowance. Higher-priced models are greyed out in the model picker with a "Sign in to use this model" tooltip; sign in to unlock them. A progress bar in the sidebar shows how much of the daily limit you've used and when it resets. When you reach the limit, MCPJam asks you to sign in.
- **Guest session limit**: if too many guest sessions start from your network in one day, a banner reads "Too many guest sessions from your network today. Sign in to continue." Signing in removes the limit.
- **Signed-in users** get **10×** the daily usage of a guest. Your daily usage and any paid credit balance are visible in the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar.
- **Paid credits** can be topped up from the billing page and are shown alongside your daily limit in the account menu.
- **Low credits warning**: when your credit balance drops to 10% or below, the sidebar credit meter turns red and shows a **Low credits** label.
- **When credits run out**, only the free hosted models are locked: they're greyed out in the model picker, so you see the limit before you send a message. Models you use with your own API key (BYOK) stay available. If you have paid top-up credits, free models stay enabled after your daily allowance is used up.

## Signing out

Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar (click your avatar or email) and choose **Sign out**. You're back in the app as a guest, with the guest allowance, until you sign in again.

OAuth tokens you connected while signed in remain in your hosted workspace and are restored the next time you sign in on the same account.

## OAuth token reveal

The hosted app stores the OAuth tokens of servers you connect. You can inspect them from the server's **Auth** tab; they're never written to `localStorage` or shared with other users.

To reveal tokens:

1. Open the **Servers** tab and click the server name to open its detail modal.
2. Go to the **Auth** tab.
3. Under **OAuth Tokens**, click **Reveal tokens**.
4. Each token (access token, refresh token, ID token) is masked by default. Click the eye icon next to a token to reveal its value, or click the copy icon to copy it directly without revealing it.

Expired tokens are refreshed before they're shown. Revealed values are cleared when you close the modal.

<Note>
  If you don't see **Reveal tokens**, disconnect and reconnect the server. The
  button appears once the server has synced to your hosted workspace.
</Note>

## What's different

The following features are not available in the hosted app. They are available when running MCPJam locally via [npx, Docker, or the desktop app](/installation).

### HTTPS only

The hosted app requires all MCP server connections to use **HTTPS**. If your server only supports HTTP, use the [local inspector](/installation) instead.

### Connections come from MCPJam's servers

The hosted app connects to your MCP server from MCPJam's servers, not from your machine. If your server only accepts known IP addresses, allow MCPJam's [published addresses](/hosted/network), or use the [local inspector](/installation), which connects from your own network.

### No STDIO connections

The hosted app can't run STDIO servers. Use a Streamable HTTP endpoint instead, or the [local inspector](/installation).

### OAuth tokens can't auto-refresh for local authorization servers

In hosted mode, the cloud backend handles token refresh on your behalf. If your MCP server's OAuth authorization server runs on a private address (localhost, a LAN IP, or a `.local`/`.internal` hostname), the cloud cannot reach it to refresh tokens after they expire.

When you connect to such a server, the app shows a warning. You can use the server until the access token expires, then you have to run the OAuth flow again. For fully local setups, use the [local inspector](/installation).

### No tunneling

Tunneling exposes a local MCP server over HTTPS. The hosted app connects only to servers that are already remote, so it has no tunneling.

### No live JSON-RPC log

The hosted app has no live log of the JSON-RPC traffic between the Inspector and your server. In the Playground, each chat turn shows its own JSON-RPC frames. For the full log, use the [local inspector](/installation).

To export eval traces from the hosted app, fetch an OTLP JSON projection of a run from `GET /api/v1/trace-exports/otlp` in the [public API](/reference/public-api). It uses OpenInference semantic conventions and loads into Arize Phoenix, Datadog or any other OTLP-compatible backend.

## Billing

### Payment history

The org billing page lists your recent credit top-ups. Each row shows:

| Column | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Date | When the top-up was processed |
| Amount | Amount charged in USD |
| Status | `Succeeded`, `Pending`, or `Failed` |
| Receipt | Link to the Stripe-hosted receipt (opens in a new tab) |

If a receipt isn't available yet the cell shows **Processing** (for pending charges) or **Not available** (for succeeded charges without a receipt link).

To add credits, use the buy-credits button on the credit balance card, or in the empty list when you have no history yet.

<Note>
  **Free plan organizations cannot purchase credits directly.** On the Free plan, the top-up dialog says only an organization owner can upgrade the plan, and offers a **Compare plans** button that opens your organization's Plans settings.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Desktop app:** Checkout opens in your default browser. Your credit balance updates in the app once the purchase completes; you don't need to return to the app from the browser.
</Note>

### Auto-reload

Auto-reload buys credits automatically when your balance runs low. It isn't
available on the Free plan. Open it
from **Auto-reload → Manage** on the credit balance card and set:

- **Credits to add**: how many credits each refill buys, from 500 to 1,000,000.
- **Minimum balance**: a refill runs when your balance falls below this.
- **Maximum monthly spend** (optional): the most auto-reload may spend in a
  month, in US dollars. Leave it blank for no limit.

The monthly limit resets each UTC calendar month. Manual top-ups don't count
toward it, and refunds don't reduce it. Saving changed settings turns
auto-reload off until you authorize the card again; saving alone never charges
you.

### Managing billing details

Organization owners on a paid plan see a **Manage billing** button on the billing page. It opens the Stripe portal, where you can update payment methods, download invoices, add tax IDs and review billing details.

### Seat payment failures

When someone you invited to a paid-seat organization signs up and the automatic seat charge fails or is cancelled, the org billing page shows a **Seat payment didn't go through** notice. As the org owner you can:

- Click **Retry payment** to reopen the charge and complete payment (including any 3DS challenge).
- Click **Remove invite** to remove the invite entirely if you no longer want to add that person.

The invited member's organization appears greyed out in their sidebar until the seat is paid.

## Good to know

- **Your server needs sign-in.** MCPJam first connects without credentials and starts OAuth if the server asks for it.
