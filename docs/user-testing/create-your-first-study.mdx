---
title: "Create your first study"
description: "Set up a hosted study for your MCP server, preview what testers see, and share it with the right audience."
icon: "plus"
---

You'll create a study for a task-board MCP server and ask testers to find blocked work before standup. To create a study, sign in to [MCPJam](https://app.mcpjam.com) as a project admin and [connect a staging server](/inspector/connecting-servers) reachable over HTTPS. Use test data that you can share with your testers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the study">
    Open **User Testing** and click **Create new study**. Give it a name, such as **Launch board usability test**, and choose the client, model, and server your testers will use. If you see **No client picked** or **No server picked**, check the corresponding selection before continuing.

    Under **Who can open it**, choose the intended audience. To collect ratings and comments on replies, enable **Turn on user ratings for every turn**. Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give testers a task">
    In **Create tasks**, add an outcome for testers to work toward:

    > You're getting ready for standup. Find the blocked tasks on the Launch board and tell us who you would follow up with for each one.

    Testers see the task list in the top right of their chat. Leave it empty if you want to see what people try on their own. Click **Create study**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the tester experience">
    From the study, click **Open preview**. Continue through the recording notice, then try the task. Check that the server responds, the instructions are visible, and testers can leave a rating if you enabled ratings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the study">
    Click **Share** and copy the tester link. If you chose **Invited users only**, add the testers' email addresses and have them sign in with those addresses. If you chose **Team members**, the link opens for signed-in team members. For a link audience, check whether the share settings require sign-in before you send it.

    Testers review a recording notice before they start. If someone can't open the link, check their sign-in state and the audience in **Share**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once testers have tried the study, see [Read study results](/user-testing/read-results) to review their sessions.
