MCPJam is an open-source platform for testing and evaluating **MCP servers, MCP Apps and ChatGPT apps**. Call your server's tools by hand or through a model, see every JSON-RPC message, and run evals to catch regressions before they ship.

## Choose your tool

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Inspector" icon="binoculars" href="/installation">
    **Test by hand.** An app in your browser, on your desktop, from your terminal or in Docker.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CLI" icon="terminal" href="/cli/overview">
    **In your shell or CI.** Connectivity, OAuth and conformance checks with JUnit or JSON output.
  </Card>
  <Card title="SDK" icon="code" href="/sdk">
    **In your test suite.** Unit tests, end-to-end tests and evals in TypeScript with Jest or Vitest.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
