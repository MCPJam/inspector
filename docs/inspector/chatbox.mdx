The **Chatbox** surface (`/chatboxes`) lets you publish and manage a host-backed chatbox for human users. Select a host from the Connect sidebar, then use the tabs below to configure and monitor it.

## Tabs

### Publish

Configure and preview the chatbox for the selected host. The split-panel view shows the configuration form on one side and a live preview on the other. Changes here control what human visitors see when they open the chatbox.

### Sessions

Browse real sessions from human visitors. Each session is a conversation thread tied to the selected host. Click a thread to inspect its messages and metadata.

The Sessions tab shows only real (non-synthetic) traffic. Synthetic sessions generated by the agent [Swarms](/inspector/swarms) surface are filtered out automatically.

**Copying a session link** — click the copy icon on a thread to get a shareable deep link. The link opens the Chatbox surface (`/chatboxes`) so the recipient lands on the human-facing view.

### Clusters

Group hosts into clusters for organizational purposes. Clusters are available on the Chatbox surface only; the [Swarms](/inspector/swarms) surface does not expose cluster management.
